Geospatial & Satellite Data Sources
 * ISRO Bhuvan OGC Services: [https://bhuvan-vec1.nrsc.gov.in/bhuvan/gwc/service/wms/](https://bhuvan-vec1.nrsc.gov.in/bhuvan/gwc/service/wms/)
   Used for live Land Use / Land Cover (LULC 50k / 10k) layers (lulc:GJ_LULC50K_1516), geomorphology, and flood risk zones.
 * Esri 10m Sentinel-2 Land Cover: [https://ic.imagery1.arcgis.com/arcgis/rest/services/Sentinel2_10m_LandCover/ImageServer/exportImage](https://ic.imagery1.arcgis.com/arcgis/rest/services/Sentinel2_10m_LandCover/ImageServer/exportImage)
   High-resolution satellite raster endpoint for real-time settlement, crop, water, and forest classification.
 * BISAG-N (Bhaskaracharya Institute): [https://maps.bisag-n.gov.in](https://maps.bisag-n.gov.in)
   Gujarat State Spatial Data Infrastructure (SSDI) for village boundaries, watershed data, and cadastral maps.
 * OpenStreetMap Nominatim Geocoding API: [https://nominatim.openstreetmap.org/search](https://nominatim.openstreetmap.org/search)
   Dynamic location, taluka, and postal code resolution bounded to Gujarat.
 * Overpass API: [https://overpass-api.de/api/interpreter](https://overpass-api.de/api/interpreter)
   Live OpenStreetMap vector feature queries for civic, natural, and water infrastructure.
 * DataMeet Spatial Repository: [https://github.com/datameet/maps](https://github.com/datameet/maps)
   Open-source boundaries for all 33 Gujarat districts, talukas, and revenue villages (Census 2011/2021).
Government Policies, Portals & Statutory Records
 * India Code (Gujarat State Enactments): [https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2462](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2462)
   Repository for the Gujarat Land Revenue Code (1879), Saurashtra Gharkhed Act (1949), and GTPUDA (1976).
 * Open Government Data (OGD) Platform India: [https://data.gov.in](https://data.gov.in)
   Live datasets for land resources, DILRMP progress metrics, and district agricultural handbooks.
 * Department of Land Resources (DoLR) - DILRMP: [https://dolr.gov.in](https://dolr.gov.in)
   Official guidelines, progress dashboards, and Modern Record Room status metrics.
 * Gujarat Revenue Department (AnyRoR / iORA): [https://anyror.gujarat.gov.in](https://anyror.gujarat.gov.in) & [https://iora.gujarat.gov.in](https://iora.gujarat.gov.in)
   Reference framework for VF-7/12 (Satbara), VF-8A, mutation entries, and Jantri circle rates.
 * MoEFCC PARIVESH Portal: [https://parivesh.nic.in](https://parivesh.nic.in)
   Eco-Sensitive Zones (ESZ), forest clearance layers, and Coastal Regulation Zone (CRZ) classifications.
 * Gujarat State Disaster Management Authority (GSDMA): [https://gsdma.org](https://gsdma.org)
   Seismic zone hazard maps and district flood vulnerability data.
Judicial & Conflict Telemetry
 * National Judicial Data Grid (NJDG): [https://njdg.ecourts.gov.in](https://njdg.ecourts.gov.in)
   District and taluka-level civil litigation pendency, land suits, and court statistics.
 * Gujarat Revenue Case Management System (RCMMS): [https://rcmms.gujarat.gov.in](https://rcmms.gujarat.gov.in)
   Appeals and dispute filings across Mamlatdar, Prant Officer, and District Collector revenue courts.
 * Land Conflict Watch Database: [https://www.landconflictwatch.org](https://www.landconflictwatch.org)
   Documented infrastructure, industrial acquisition, and forest rights disputes across Gujarat.
Web GIS & Cartography Tooling
 * MapLibre GL JS: [https://unpkg.com/maplibre-gl@4.7.1/dist/maplibre-gl.js](https://unpkg.com/maplibre-gl@4.7.1/dist/maplibre-gl.js) & [https://maplibre.org/](https://maplibre.org/)
   Primary hardware-accelerated WebGL vector and raster map engine.
 * CartoDB Positron Style: [https://basemaps.cartocdn.com/gl/positron-gl-style/style.json](https://basemaps.cartocdn.com/gl/positron-gl-style/style.json)
   High-contrast, muted vector base style that allows thematic LULC overlays to stand out cleanly.
 * Leaflet JS: [https://unpkg.com/leaflet@1.9.4/dist/leaflet.js](https://unpkg.com/leaflet@1.9.4/dist/leaflet.js)
   Quick-start lightweight mapping library used in early prototyping.
 * Kepler.gl: [https://kepler.gl/](https://kepler.gl/)
   Reference engine for large-scale geospatial intelligence dashboards and GPU-driven rendering.
 * Protomaps / PMTiles: [https://protomaps.com/](https://protomaps.com/) & [https://github.com/protomaps/PMTiles](https://github.com/protomaps/PMTiles)
   Serverless, single-file vector tile specification for large cadastral maps.
 * Tippecanoe: [https://github.com/felt/tippecanoe](https://github.com/felt/tippecanoe)
   Mapbox/Felt vector tile generator used to convert state-level GeoJSON into zoom-scaled tiles.
